Privacy Policy

App: Tsumiki · Provider: me LLC (合同会社me) · Last updated: October 6, 2026

日本語

Thank you for using Tsumiki (the “App”). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect your personal information. It does not obtain your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or health data. No account registration is required.

Everything you enter or record in the App — habit names, icons, colors, frequencies, times of day, reminder times, completion history, and so on — is stored only on your device. There is no developer server, and none of this is transmitted anywhere automatically.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

The App's data is stored on your device in a shared app group container (group.com.divme.tsumiki) in SwiftData (SQLite) format. The following read from and write to this container:

  • The Tsumiki app itself
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • The Control Center control
  • Actions run from Shortcuts and Siri

All of this happens on the same device. The App has no app-managed iCloud sync. Apple's handling of data through device backup, Siri, and other system services depends on your settings and Apple's policies.

3. When Data Leaves Your Device

The App never transmits data automatically. Data leaves your device only in the following cases, each of which requires an explicit action by you:

  • Exporting your data: exporting to CSV, or exporting a backup (JSON), from the Settings screen opens the iOS share sheet. CSV includes active habit names and completion timestamps. JSON backups include settings, completion timestamps, notes, and the home time zone, including archived habits. This data is passed to the destination you choose (mail, notes, files, a third-party cloud service, and so on). Once there, the data is handled under that service's own terms.
  • Sharing an achievement: when you reach a milestone you can post it via the share sheet. The shared text contains only your streak length; it does not include habit names.
  • Sharing your year in review (Premium): from the Statistics screen you can export a heatmap image of your past year and share it via the share sheet. The image shows daily completion rates in a colour grid, the number of days recorded, and your current streak; it does not include habit names.
  • External links: opening the App Store review page, this policy, or the Terms of Use takes you to your browser or the App Store.

4. Apple Services

  • In-app purchase (StoreKit): buying and restoring Premium is handled through Apple. All the App receives from Apple is whether a purchase exists; it never obtains or stores credit card details or your Apple ID.
  • Local notifications: all notifications are generated on your device. They do not pass through any external server.
  • Shortcuts and Siri: habit names and identifiers are provided to the system so they can be selected or spoken in Shortcuts and Siri. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Widgets: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets display habit names. If you place a Lock Screen widget, those names may appear on screen without unlocking your device.

Apple's privacy policy is available at https://www.apple.com/privacy/.

5. Notifications

The App sends four kinds of notification. All are local notifications, and their contents may include habit names and streak lengths.

  • Per-habit reminders (at the time you set)
  • A weekly summary (Sundays at 20:00)
  • An evening reminder (21:00, only for habits you enable it on)
  • A warning when a streak is about to break (20:30)

If you do not grant notification permission, every other feature of the App remains available.

6. Permissions

The only system permission the App requests is notifications. It never requests location, photos, contacts, camera, microphone, health, or tracking access.

7. Settings Stored on Your Device

Alongside your records, the following settings and states are stored on your device. None of them are transmitted anywhere.

  • Your theme, text size, background motion, and frosted-glass settings
  • Notes you attached to completed days (only if you entered any)
  • A time zone identifier (the App records your time zone at first launch so that travel does not throw off your streaks)
  • Whether onboarding is complete, and which milestone animations have been shown
  • When the Premium upgrade prompt and the review request were last shown (so they are not repeated excessively)
  • Your Premium purchase state (cached so it can be shared with the widget)

8. Deleting and Backing Up Data

Uninstalling the App deletes all of the data described above. You can also erase every record yourself with “Delete All Records” in Settings. The developer has no means of accessing your data.

The App does not support iCloud sync. Your records do not carry over automatically when you change devices or reset your device. If you export a backup from the Settings screen, you can bring it back with “Restore from Backup” on the new device. Restoring is free. CSV export is write-only and cannot be used to restore.

9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App uses no analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. It embeds no third-party software development kits (SDKs). It performs no tracking across devices or across apps.

10. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information, it is safe to use at any age. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Significant changes will be announced through an app update.
