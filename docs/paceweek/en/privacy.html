Privacy Policy

Service: PaceWeek / Last updated: September 23, 2026

me LLC ("we") explains how PaceWeek ("the Service") handles information. This policy describes the current prototype; it will be updated when the Service adds network, account, analytics, or billing features.

1. Information stored

The prototype does not require an account. Habit names, goals, schedules, time and energy choices, records, and reflection notes are stored locally on the device or in the browser. They are not automatically sent to our servers.

2. Information not collected

The prototype does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. It has no ads, analytics SDK, in-app AI, or cloud sync.

3. Backups and copying

If you choose to display or copy a JSON backup, the data is shown in the app and passed to your device clipboard. We do not receive the copied content. Check the destination before sharing records that may contain personal information.

4. Apple services

If App Store distribution or subscriptions are connected in the future, purchase processing will use Apple services. The current prototype has no connected billing. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or the app/browser data is removed. Device failure, uninstalling the app, or clearing browser data may erase it. Back up data before deletion when needed.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Service changes. Questions can be sent to contact@div-me.com.
