Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can start using the App right after installing it. Your data is stored only on your device.

Does my data sync across devices?

Automatic sync between devices isn't supported yet. When switching devices, use "Export data" in Settings to save a JSON file, then "Import" it on the new device. Premium users can also use automatic iCloud backup.

Is Premium a subscription?

No — it's a one-time purchase. There is no recurring charge.

I got a new device. Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings.

I'm not receiving notifications

Please check the following:

  • Notifications are allowed under iOS Settings → Notifications → StockSnap
  • "Expiry alerts" is turned on in the App's Settings screen
  • Focus or Do Not Disturb is not active

The "days left" count looks off

This was fixed in v3.9.3, which switched to calendar-day based calculation. Please update to the latest version.

How do I switch the display language?

Tap the gear icon at the top right of the inventory tab to open Settings, then choose your language under Display → "言語 / Language".

How do I delete my data?

Delete items individually in the App, or uninstall the App to remove all data.
